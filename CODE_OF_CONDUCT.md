# Code of Conduct

Servexa Warranty AI is an open-source AI warranty platform. We want the community to be useful, rigorous, and safe for contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, then ask clarifying questions when something is unclear.
- Give technical feedback that is specific, actionable, and tied to the work.
- Credit ideas, reports, research, and prior art.
- Treat sensitive customer information, prompt logic, and proprietary manuals carefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruption of issues, pull requests, discussions, or reviews.
- Sharing customer PII, proprietary warranty manuals, or prompt injection exploits in public when responsible disclosure is required.
- Encouraging unauthorized testing or misuse of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, limit participation, or block users when behavior harms the project or community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting contributors and users.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, please contact the maintainers by opening an issue or discussion on the repository, providing only the details needed to route the report. For sensitive conduct concerns or reports that include private information, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or direct messages to maintainers where available, and avoid posting personal details publicly.
